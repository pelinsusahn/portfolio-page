AN ARCHITECT WORKING BETWEEN SPATIAL DESIGN, SYSTEMS, MATERIALS, AND VISUAL STORYTELLING.

I am an architect working between spatial design, computational systems, material experiments, and visual storytelling. My work often starts from a simple question: what happens when architecture is treated less like a final object and more like a system that can learn, deform, fail, adapt, or be reused?

I move between prototypes, drawings, datasets, machines, and speculative worlds because they are different ways of testing architectural ideas, not separate disciplines.
